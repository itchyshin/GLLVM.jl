## 1. Goal
Advance the finite Core070 contract by connecting the frozen family admission
facts to concrete, testable Julia calls without confusing entry checks with fits.

## 2. Implemented
The new family-route contract accounts for69 frozen facts:21 required,1 alias,
33 rejected and14 constructor-only exclusions. It names22 Julia calls, their
model distinctions and existing fitted-fixture leads. All formula/bridge and
fitted-acceptance fields remain explicitly unpaid. The full frozen manifest
and required source-case map are not promoted by this supplement.

## 3a. Decisions and Rejected Alternatives
Preserve the exact frozen reference. One-part lognormal uses shared sigma;
delta-lognormal requires per-trait positive scales. Gamma's generic shared
shape is not its grouped default-R comparison. Tweedie per-trait estimated
power, shared estimated power and fixed power are different models. Student
finite low-df/vector support is a Julia extension, not an R rejection to copy.
Native multinomial fixed effects cannot stand in for latent/structured support.
